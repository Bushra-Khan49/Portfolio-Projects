Pyrimidine Substitution

Uracil vs. Thymine

The Fourth Base

While both molecules utilize Adenine, Cytosine, and Guanine, the fourth base changes depending on the nucleic acid type.

DNA uses Thymine (5-methyluracil), whereas RNA uses Uracil.

The Chemical Difference

The chemical difference is a single methyl group (-CH₃) at the C5 position of Thymine, which is absent in Uracil.
