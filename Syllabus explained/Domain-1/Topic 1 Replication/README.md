# 🧬 Domain 1 · Topic 1 — Replication

An interactive, scroll-driven 3D visualization exploring the process of DNA replication and its connections to Bioinformatics.

## 🌐 Live Demo

**[View Live Site →](https://bushra-khan49.github.io/Portfolio-Projects/Syllabus%20explained/Domain-1/Topic%201%20Replication/index.html)**

---

## 📖 What's Covered

| Section | Topics |
|---------|--------|
| **Importance** | Why accurate replication matters for cells and bioinformatics |
| **Replication Machinery** | Helicase, Primase, Polymerase, Ligase, Topoisomerase, Clamp |
| **Mechanics** | Semi-conservative and bidirectional replication (forks) |
| **Directionality** | 5' &rarr; 3' synthesis and the need for a free 3'-OH group |
| **Strand Asymmetry** | Leading vs Lagging strand (Okazaki fragments) |
| **Bioinformatics Connections**| Read mapping, variant calling, and interpreting sequencing errors |

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Three.js** — 3D animated DNA helix in the background
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** — Scroll-driven card animations
- **Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS** — No frameworks, fast and lightweight
