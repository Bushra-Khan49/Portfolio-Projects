# 🤝 Code of Conduct: CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md

## 1. Our Commitment
In the interest of fostering an open, collaborative, and welcoming scientific environment, we as contributors and maintainers commit to making participation in the **Nexus Genomics Institute** project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## 2. Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and scientific methodologies.
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism.
- Focusing on what is best for the scientific community.
- Showing empathy towards other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Personal or political attacks.
- Harassment or discrimination.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 3. Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior. They will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behavior.

## 4. Reporting
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the project lead, **Bushra Khan**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and discreetly.
