# 🗺️ Herbal Omics Lab: The Roadmap

This document outlines the planned trajectory for the development and expansion of the Herbal Omics Lab digital platform.

---

## 📅 Currently Implemented
- [x] **v1.0**: Custom JSON CMS infrastructure.
- [x] **v1.1**: Secure PI Admin Dashboard.
- [x] **v1.2**: Real-time "Live Sync" engine.
- [x] **v1.3**: Premium high-contrast dark mode.
- [x] **v1.4**: Unified Team & PI profile management.

---

## 🚀 Short-Term Goals (Next 3–6 Months)
- [ ] **Automated Bibliography Sync**: Integrate with Zotero or Mendeley for one-click publications updates.
- [ ] **Interactive Equipment Viewer**: A 3D/Interactive section for laboratory facilities and equipment specifications.
- [ ] **Secure File Sharing**: A private area within the Admin panel for lab members to share internal research papers.

---

## 📡 Long-Term Vision
- [ ] **Interactive Lab Progress Graph**: A real-time data visualization of ongoing research projects and completion percentages.
- [ ] **Community Blog**: A centralized space for lab members to publish insights and updates.
- [ ] **Grant & Funding Workspace**: An internal tracker for applications and laboratory resources.
